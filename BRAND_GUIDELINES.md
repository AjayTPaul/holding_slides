# Brand Guidelines & Project Standards
- **Strictly adhere to these design rules when generating CSS/HTML.**

Brand Guidelines
1. Typography
Our brand is digital-first. Use the primary fonts for all communications (except Microsoft applications, where Calibri is the default).
Aleo (Headlines Only)
Role: Characterful headline typeface. Do not use for paragraph copy.
Style: Contemporary slab serif, warm, semi-rounded details.
Weights: Light, Regular.
Open Sans (Body Copy & Alt Titles)
Role: General documents, material copy, and body text. Can be used as an alternative title font.
Style: Humanist sans serif, neutral, friendly, highly legible.
Weights: Light, Regular, Bold (Use Regular for body copy; Bold only for highlighting words).

3. Gradients
Versatile elements used to add depth and dimension. Adhere strictly to these construction rules:
Angle: 45° (Lightest color pointing upwards).
Start/End Points: 0% and 100%.
Transition Point: Set exactly at 25%.
Dominance: The darkest color must be dominant.
Note: Only use officially approved brand color combinations.
4. Layout & Structure
The Grid (Rule of Thirds): Divide compositions into multiples of three (horizontally, vertically, or both). This grid is flexible and scales across dimensions.
Hierarchy: Use asymmetric layouts to establish hierarchy. Plan the movement of the eye to guide the viewer logically through the narrative.
Let It Breathe (Whitespace): Never overcrowd the canvas. Use generous whitespace to create visual pauses, group/separate elements, and make layouts clean, approachable, and easy to understand.
